# ForeFlux

**ForeFlux is an inventory and demand planning tool for small businesses with physical stock.**

Most small business owners run inventory on gut feel and spreadsheets. They over-order what doesn't sell, run out of what does, and find out at month's end. ForeFlux reads live sales data and tells owners exactly what they have, what they'll need, and when to reorder — no analyst required.
